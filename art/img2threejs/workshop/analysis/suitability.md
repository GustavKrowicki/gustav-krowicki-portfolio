# Suitability

Verdict: PASS, stylised game prop (cut-away room diorama).
- The phone photos are an interior scene (the rubric's reject case on their own). The primary reference is the
  AI Studio isometric cut-away made from them, which shows the room as one object from the game camera.
- Everything is hard-surface: boxes and cylinders. Silhouette gates compare the lab's iso view (30 deg, yaw 45,
  cropped to the model) with that image.
- Limits: whiteboard text is stylised into unreadable strokes (public repo); people and the ceiling are left out;
  hidden walls are not modelled; sprite scale (~62 px per wu) drops sub-pixel props.
