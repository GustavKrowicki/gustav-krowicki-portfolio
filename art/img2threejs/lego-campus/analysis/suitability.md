# Suitability

Verdict: SUITABLE (stylised game prop).
- The AI image is a clean, flat-shaded isometric illustration: hard-surface boxes, extrusions,
  cylinders and flat colours, which procedural Three.js reproduces well.
- Structure is not guessed from the single view: footprints and heights are measured from the Krea
  GLB plan (the model the live sprite was made from), so proportions and sprite offset can be held.
- Limits: hidden sides are inferred; window and perforation detail is stylised into bands at the
  sprite scale (~230 px per Krea unit; one floor ~ 6 px).
