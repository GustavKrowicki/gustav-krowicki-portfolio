## Key takeaways

1. **Concept works** — visually stands out, fun idea, playful vs. direct path choice is good
2. **Hotspot click needs immediate feedback** — clicking rotating hotspots should visibly confirm the interaction
3. **Auto-show info on arrival** — show the info box when the player reaches the building instead of requiring spacebar
4. **Pixelation** — noted, but this is an intentional design choice I'm keeping
5. **Car pathing** — some cars drive in odd directions
6. **More case work in the city** — surface more project/case content directly
